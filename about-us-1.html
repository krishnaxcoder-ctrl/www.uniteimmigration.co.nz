I’m Jasmine, a Licensed Immigration Adviser and the founder of Unite Immigration. Helping people make New Zealand their home is not just my profession - it is my passion.

At Unite, we see immigration as more than forms and checklists. It’s about people, families, and futures. Behind every application are real goals and aspirations, and we approach each case with dignity, clarity, and care.

We provide advice and support across all major visa categories, including work, student, residence, partnership, and complex matters. Our approach is guided by empathy, ethical practice, and respect - with a commitment to delivering advice that is
practical, responsive, and focused on successful outcomes.

Most importantly, we never lose sight of the people behind the process. You are not just a case file or number - you are a person with a story that matters, and our role is to help you build the future you envision in New Zealand.
