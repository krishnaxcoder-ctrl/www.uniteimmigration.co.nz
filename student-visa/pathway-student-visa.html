Planning multiple NZ courses? Study up to 3 courses on a single visa - ideal for students following a planned study pathway with one or more approved education providers.

Minimum Requirements

Admission offer: Hold a valid pathway offer of place from an approved pathway education provider(s)

Sufficient funds: Have enough money to pay tuition fees, live on or an acceptable sponsor

Insurance: Hold full medical and travel insurance for the length of stay

Health & Character: You must be of good character and in good health.

Intention to leave: Genuinely intend to study and to leave NZ at the end of your stay

Overview of Pathway Student Visa

Our licensed immigration experts study your case carefully and put together a foolproof application to maximize your chances of success. We uncover any hidden issues with your Pathway Student Visa application upfront to reduce rework and keep your costs down while avoiding delays.

Visa benefits

This visa allows you to:

Study up to 3 courses, one after another, on a single visa

Work up to 25 hours/week during study and full-time in scheduled holidays

Multiple entry travel while your visa is valid

A single, streamlined visa covering your full qualification plan

Visa conditions

Study Rights:

You cannot include your partner or dependent children - they may apply for visas based on their relationship to you

You must maintain insurance, meet attendance/progress, and satisfy prerequisites for 2nd/3rd courses to remain eligible

Work rights:

If 16-17 (or in Years 12–13), written permission from parents/guardian and education provider is required

Full-time in scheduled holidays requires course/holiday evidence (e.g., 120 credits and holiday dates)

Practical work experience must be a course requirement and separately evidenced (does not count toward work-hour limits)

Funds:

Show payment for your first course/first year and credible means for the rest of the pathway (savings, scholarship, approved loan, etc.)

Health/character:

X-ray/medical and police certificates may be required based on stay length, age, and travel history

You must hold evidence of onward travel or funds to purchase a ticket when your studyends

FAQs

What is a Pathway Student Visa?

It allows you to study up to 3 courses in sequence on one visa with approved New Zealand education providers.

How long can I stay in New Zealand?

You can stay for up to 5 years, depending on your study pathway and passport validity.

Can I work on a Pathway Student Visa?

Yes. Most students can work up to 25 hours per week and full-time during holidays, if eligible.

How much money do I need to show?

You must show funds for tuition fees and living costs - usually NZD $20,000 per year.

When should I apply?

Apply online only and ideally at least 3 months before travel, especially during peak periods (Oct–Mar).
