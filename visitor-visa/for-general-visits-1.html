Minimum Requirements

Genuine Intentions: You must have genuine reasons for coming to New Zealand.

Sufficient Funds: You must have enough money to pay your living expenses or have an acceptable sponsor

Valid Passport: Ensure your passport is valid for at least three months beyond your intended departure date.

No Employment: You must not plan to work in New Zealand, unless you work remotely for a business overseas

Health & Character: You must be of good character and in good health.

Intention to Leave: Intend to leave New Zealand at the end of your stay

An overview of General Visitor Visa in New Zealand

The New Zealand General Visitor Visa opens the door to breathtaking landscapes, vibrant cities, and rich cultural heritage. Whether you're visiting friends and family, attending business meetings, or exploring natural wonders, this visa lets you experience all that New Zealand has to offer.

FAQs

What is a New Zealand Visitor Visa?

A New Zealand visitor visa allows travellers to enter the country for short-term courses, family vacations, or tourism. This visa typically permits stays of up to nine months during an eighteen-month period, depending on your eligibility and travel purpose. It's ideal for tourists wanting to explore New Zealand's attractions or visit family members. Application requirements vary by nationality, with factors such as financial stability and travel history also considered during the assessment process.

Can I bring my family on a Visitor Visa to New Zealand?

You can add your partner and children (age 19 or under) in your visitor visa to accompany you to New Zealand. Each family member must meet. Each family member must meet financial, character, and health requirements.

What is the visa fee for a New Zealand Visitor Visa?

The New Zealand Visitor Visa fee depends on the applicant's nationality and where they submit their application. Online application costs typically range from NZD $441 to $646, though prices may vary based on payment method or currency exchange rates. Prospective visitors should verify the current processing fees on the New Zealand Immigration website before applying.

How much funding is required for a New Zealand Visitor Visa?

Applicants must demonstrate financial self-sufficiency by showing at least NZ$1,000 per month, or NZ$400 per month if accommodation is prepaid. Acceptable proof includes traveler's checks, and sponsorship letters.

Can I work in NewZealand while on a Visitor Visa?

No, a visitor visa to New Zealand does not permit employment. This visa only allows tourism, brief study, and visiting family and friends. If you're seeking work opportunities, consider applying for a work visa that matches your qualifications and skills. Working without authorization may result in visa cancellation and possible bans from future entry.

What are the medical requirements for a New Zealand Visitor Visa?

Applicants planning to stay longer than six months may need a chest X-ray or medical certificate to verify their health. This helps Immigration New Zealand confirm that visitors pose no risk to public health. Routine medical examinations might be required based on age, health history, and nationality. Preparing medical documentation before applying can streamline processing and prevent delays.

Can I study in New Zealand on a Visitor Visa?

Yes, you can study in New Zealand for up to three months while on a visitor visa. This is perfect for tourists who want to complete a short course or learn a new skill without needing a full student visa. However, longer study periods require a New Zealand student visa, and some course providers may request additional documentation for enrollment, such as proof of language proficiency.
