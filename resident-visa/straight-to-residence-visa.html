About this Visa

The Straight to Residence Visa is designed for migrants working in critical, highly skilled roles that New Zealand urgently needs. If your occupation appears on Tier 1 of the GreenList, you may be eligible to apply for residence immediately, without waiting to complete time in New Zealand.

Applicants must have a job or job oﬀer from an accredited employer and meet all GreenList, employment, health, character, and English language requirements.

Minimum Requirements

Age Limit: You must be 55 years or younger

Work Experience : Are working in, or have a job oﬀer for, a Green List Tier 1 role

Employment: Have a full-time role with an accredited employer

Meet income requirements: Meet the pay, qualification, registration, or experience requirements for their GreenList occupation

Language Obligation: They must understand and speak English fluently or pay for lessons

Health and Character Requirements: They must meet health and character requirements

Other Requirements: They must meet the other requirements of their pathway visa.

Visa Conditions

Immigration New Zealand carefully assesses whether your role genuinely meets Green List Tier 1 criteria. This involves more than confirming your job title. Key Green List Tier 1 employment requirements include.

Employment with an accredited employer

A role listed on Tier 1 of the Green List

Full-time employment (at least 30 hours per week)

Ongoing employment:

  • Permanent, or
  • Fixed-term for at least 12 months, or
  • Contract or continuous contracts of at least 6 months

Wages paid at:

  • The specific Green List rate for your role, or
  • At least the median wage, where no specific rate is listed

You must also meet any role-specific Green List requirements, such as:

  • Occupational registration
  • Professional membership
  • Minimum qualifications or experience

Contractors and contracts for services

If your role is based on a contract for services, additional requirements may apply. Depending on the Green List role, this can include:

Evidence of significant relevant experience (often up to 10 years), and

Pay meeting a higher contract-for-services wage threshold

Each Green List role has its own requirements, and careful assessment is essential.

Qualifications Required by INZ

If your qualifications were obtained overseas, you may need an International Qualification Assessment (IQA) from NZQA.

Some international qualifications are exempt, such as those recognised under the Washington Accord or Sydney Accord.

English language requirements

You must show that you can speak and understand English, usually through:

An approved English language test, or

Evidence of eligible citizenship/study/work background

Partners and dependent children aged 16 or older must also meet English requirements or, where permitted, pre-purchase English language tuition.

Family inclusion

You may include:

Your partner

Your dependent children aged 24 or younger

All included family members must meet health, character, and (where applicable) English language requirements.

FAQs

Is this a direct pathway to residence?

Yes. This visa gives you New Zealand residence immediately if you meet the Green List Tier 1 requirements.

Do I need to work in New Zealand first?

No. You can apply with a job offer from an accredited employer, even if you are
offshore.

What kind of job do I need?

Your role must be full-time, Tier 1 on the Green List, and with an accredited
employer, meeting the required salary and role conditions.

Can my partner and children be included?

Yes. You can include your partner and dependent children (up to 24 years old) in the same residence application.

What happens after the visa is granted?

You can live, work, and study in New Zealand indefinitely, and apply for Permanent Residence after 2 years.
